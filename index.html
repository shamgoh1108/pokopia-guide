🗺️ Story checklist

There are 4 story areas. Start in Withered Wasteland, then do Bleak Beach and Rocky Ridges in either order, and finish with Sparkling Skylands. Every area follows the same pattern: rebuild its Pokémon Center (needs Environment Level 3), then finish its big request.

Gold boxes show the must-have Pokémon, moves and food for each area.

Area 1

Withered Wasteland

Must-haves
  • Squirtle → Water Gun, Bulbasaur → Leafage, Scyther → Cut, Hitmonchan → Rock Smash, Drilbur → Rototiller (and free Bean Seeds)
  • Timburr or Machop as builders (4 tall grass + 1 big boulder)
  • Slowpoke for the story (4 tall grass + 2 ocean water)
  • Charmander to light fires and ovens
Area 2 (or 3)

Bleak Beach

Must-haves
  • Lapras → Surf (1 big palm tree + 4 hedges + 2 ocean water). Lapras may only show up after you leave and come back.
  • Zorua → Camouflage (4 seashore flowers)
  • Pawmi for electricity (4 seashore flowers, or a seat + table + menu board + plated food)
  • Torchic (wooden birdhouse + table + plated food) and Piplup (4 yellow tall grass + 2 water)
  • Trubbish (waste bin + sign + garbage bags). It turns trash into iron and paper.
Area 3 (or 2)

Rocky Ridges

Must-haves
  • Machoke → Strength. Graveler → Rollout (4 moss + 1 mossy boulder)
  • Chef Dente: cooking is a huge help from here on
  • Ludicolo / Lotad for the party (4 duckweed + 2 water)
  • Start growing Wheat and Beans now, because the party needs 15 of each
Area 4 (last)

Sparkling Skylands

Opens after you've met Kyogre, Raikou and Volcanion.

Must-haves
  • Dragonite → Glide. Gyarados → Waterfall (a better Surf)
  • Froakie (4 pink tall grass + 2 water)
  • Lots of builders: the floors need 6, then 10, then 15 Pokémon helping
  • Save up iron, copper, paper and bricks before you start (see Materials)

        🌱 First steps
        • Change the camera: in Settings, set the camera distance to Far so you can see more while building.
        • Try Mouse Mode: turn it on in Settings, then lay the right Joy-Con flat on a table and use it like a mouse. It makes placing blocks much easier.
        • Placing blocks: hold A to keep placing blocks in a row. Hold ZL to walk sideways while you place, which makes straight walls easy.
        • The day resets at 5 AM. Shop stock, Mosslax's buff and your Dream Island trip all refresh then.
        • Pick up everything: press Y to grab small bits on the ground. Wood scraps, stones and seeds add up fast.
        Tip: Learn new moves and follow the story before spending a lot of time decorating. Each move opens up new things to do.

        🌾 Farming
        1. Help Drilbur in eastern Withered Wasteland. It teaches Rototiller and gives you 5 Bean Seeds.
        2. Use Rototiller on grass or dirt to make a farm plot.
        3. Hold the seeds from your bag, aim at the plot and press A to plant them.
        4. Water them with Water Gun if they need it, then wait.
        CropWhere to get seedsNotes
        BeansDrilbur, Pokémon Center shopEasiest crop, no watering needed. Used for hamburger steaks.
        WheatShop, gifts from Rocky Ridges Pokémon, red Poké BallsUsed for bread. You need lots for the Rocky Ridges party.
        TomatoesUnlocks at Bleak Beach Environment Level 3Need watering.
        Tip: Keep a Grow Pokémon (Bulbasaur, Rowlet) near your farm to make crops grow faster. Rock Smash on wild crops gives you their seeds.

        🏡 Homes & Pokémon helpers
        • Building kits are sold in the PC Shop inside any Pokémon Center. Place the kit, add the materials it needs, and have a Build Pokémon (like Machop or Timburr) build it.
        • You can also build your own house: walls at least 2 blocks high, a door, and at least 3 pieces of furniture. Up to 4 Pokémon can share a house.

        😊 Making Pokémon happy

        Happy Pokémon raise the area's Environment Level, which unlocks new shop items and moves the story along. You need level 5 in all 4 areas to finish the game.

        See what a Pokémon likes

        Open the Pokédex, pick the Pokémon and go to the "Specialties and Likes" tab. It lists their favourite things (like "Cute stuff" or "Wooden stuff") and their favourite food flavour.

        Quick ways to raise happiness

        • Help the saddest Pokémon first. Often a berry, a toy or a bed is enough to cheer one up.
        • Move them into proper houses. Houses make them happier than open habitats.
        • Decorate with their favourite things, and remove anything they dislike.
        • Feed them dishes in their favourite flavour. This works much better than raw food.
        • Do their requests and play their mini-games.
        • Turn dry mud into grass with Water Gun and Leafage. Greener land raises the level too.

        🎀 Decorating ideas
        • Decorate to their taste: check each Pokémon's likes and theme their house around them, for example a cosy wooden cottage or a cute pastel room. It looks lovely and makes them happier.
        • Use habitats as decorations: a picnic set (2 seats, table, picnic basket) makes a cute garden corner and attracts Pikachu. A restaurant setup (seat, table, menu board, plated food) makes a café and attracts Pawmi.
        • Add lights everywhere: they make things feel cosy, and some habitats need a light.
        • Furniture works indoors and outdoors. Benches, lamps and flower pots along paths make a town feel lived-in.
        • Recolour things: later on you can paint items to match a colour theme.
        • Shop stock changes daily, so check the PC Shop often for new furniture.

        🪙 Life Coins (money)
        • The easiest way to earn coins is Challenges on the Pokémon Center PC. Remember to go back to the PC to collect your reward.
        • Do the Stamp Rally: get one stamp a day from a PC, then trade in the full card for a big pile of coins.
        • Crafted items sell for more than raw materials.
        • Good first buy: Packing Tips from the shop, which give you a bigger bag. New ones appear as you unlock new areas.

        🍞 Cooking with Chef Dente

        Rescuing Chef Dente (Rocky Ridges)

        1. Collect 3 Wheat from the dry patches near the waterfall.
        2. Bake Simple Bread in a Bread Oven. You need a fire Pokémon (Burn helper) to light the oven.
        3. Eat the bread to power up Cut, then cut the chain holding his barrel.

        How food works

        Eating a cooked dish refills all your PP and powers up one move:

        FoodCooked onMain ingredientPowers up
        SaladCutting BoardLeafLeafage (grows grass in a + shape)
        SoupCooking PotFresh WaterWater Gun (bigger spray)
        BreadBread OvenWheatCut (cuts tougher things, from further away)
        Hamburger SteakFrying PanBeanRock Smash (breaks hard rock, gets gold and iron)
        Recipes worth knowing
        • Simple Salad: leaf + anything
        • Simple Soup: fresh water + any 2
        • Simple Bread: wheat + any 2. Needed to free Chef Dente.
        • Simple Hamburger Steak: bean + any 3. Needed to break into the legendary caves.
        • Vibrant Hamburger Steak: bean + potato + salad + any. Gives a bigger Rock Smash boost.
        Tip: Keep a couple of dishes in your bag at all times. They're your emergency PP refill.

        🍽️ Mosslax's daily treat

        After the Bleak Beach story, you can leave one dish a day at Mosslax's altar. The dish's flavour decides what bonus you get for the rest of the day:

        • Spicy: more Pokémon show up in habitats (great when hunting new ones)
        • Sweet: find more ancient artefacts when digging
        • Bitter: more rare items
        • Dry: more rare bird Pokémon (Mosslax's favourite)
        • Sour: better items in the shop
        • Plain: make friends with Pokémon faster

        🎈 Dream Islands

        These are bonus islands full of materials, with a small chance of legendary Pokémon.

        1. Find Drifloon on the west beach of Withered Wasteland.
        2. Make its habitat: place 3 campfires side by side and have a fire Pokémon light them.
        3. To travel, put a doll on the ground and look at it. Drifloon flies you to that doll's island.

        Legendaries are rare. Some hide in caves you have to break into, so eat a Hamburger Steak first to power up Rock Smash.

        🏁 Getting ready for the ending

        The final task is the Team Initiation Challenge: 9 deliveries in a row. Tick things off as you collect them while you play, to save a lot of grinding at the end.
