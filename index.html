🗺️ Story checklist

There are 4 story areas. Start in Withered Wasteland, then do Bleak Beach and Rocky Ridges in either order, and finish with Sparkling Skylands. Every area follows the same pattern: rebuild its Pokémon Center (needs Environment Level 3), then finish its big request.

Gold boxes show the must-have Pokémon, moves and food for each area.

Area 1

Withered Wasteland

Must-haves
  • Squirtle → Water Gun, Bulbasaur → Leafage, Scyther → Cut, Hitmonchan → Rock Smash, Drilbur → Rototiller (and free Bean Seeds)
  • Timburr or Machop as builders (4 tall grass + 1 big boulder)
  • Slowpoke for the story (4 tall grass + 2 ocean water)
  • Charmander to light fires and ovens
Area 2 (or 3)

Bleak Beach

Must-haves
  • Lapras → Surf (1 big palm tree + 4 hedges + 2 ocean water). Lapras may only show up after you leave and come back.
  • Zorua → Camouflage (4 seashore flowers)
  • Pawmi for electricity (4 seashore flowers, or a seat + table + menu board + plated food)
  • Torchic (wooden birdhouse + table + plated food) and Piplup (4 yellow tall grass + 2 water)
  • Trubbish (waste bin + sign + garbage bags). It turns trash into iron and paper.
Area 3 (or 2)

Rocky Ridges

Must-haves
  • Machoke → Strength. Graveler → Rollout (4 moss + 1 mossy boulder)
  • Chef Dente: cooking is a huge help from here on
  • Ludicolo / Lotad for the party (4 duckweed + 2 water)
  • Start growing Wheat and Beans now, because the party needs 15 of each
Area 4 (last)

Sparkling Skylands

Opens after you've met Kyogre, Raikou and Volcanion.

Must-haves
  • Dragonite → Glide. Gyarados → Waterfall (a better Surf)
  • Froakie (4 pink tall grass + 2 water)
  • Lots of builders: the floors need 6, then 10, then 15 Pokémon helping
  • Save up iron, copper, paper and bricks before you start (see Materials)

        …get more tools and things to craft?

        • Your first Workbench costs just 2 Stone. Put it near home.
        • New crafting recipes unlock when you:
          • progress the story
          • raise an area's Environment Level
          • finish Pokémon requests
          • open glowing Poké Balls you find while exploring
          • check sparkling ripples in water
          • buy them in the PC Shop, which has daily specials
        • Cooking tools (Cutting Board, Cooking Pot, Bread Oven, Frying Pan) unlock one by one after rescuing Chef Dente. Make them at the Workbench.
        • Sprinkler (waters crops for you): look for it in the shop or as a daily special.
        • Your moves are your real tools, so meet new Pokémon to learn more. See Moves.

        …swim?

        1. In Bleak Beach, build Lapras's habitat: 1 big palm tree + 4 hedges + 2 ocean water. Leave the area and come back if it doesn't show up right away.
        2. Lapras asks for help cleaning mud off the beach. Bring a Water Pokémon like Piplup to spray it away.
        3. Lapras then teaches you Surf. Press B in water to swim.
        • Swimming uses PP, so bring snacks.
        • Waterfall (from Gyarados in Skylands) lets you go up waterfalls.
        • Dive (underwater): update the game to 2.0, get Bleak Beach to Environment Level 5, and make sure you know Jump and Surf. Then talk to Professor Tangrowth, go to the new island, and bring Manaphy 5 Seaweed.

        …attract a specific Pokémon?

        1. Find its habitat in the Habitats lists, or in the Habitat Dex in your Pokédex.
        2. Build it in the right area. Some need water, ocean water or a high spot.
        3. If something else already lives there, move that Pokémon into a house to free up the spot.
        4. Rare ones (★★ or ★★★) take longer. Feed Mosslax a Spicy dish to make Pokémon show up more often.
        5. Some only come out at certain times of day, so check in the morning and again at night.
        6. Already seen it but can't find it? Use the Fly trick in Tricks.

        …build a super productive area?

        See Busy-bee setups: Pokémon that make items, paired with Pokémon that collect them.

        🌱 First steps
        • Change the camera: in Settings, set the camera distance to Far so you can see more while building.
        • Try Mouse Mode: turn it on in Settings, then lay the right Joy-Con flat on a table and use it like a mouse. It makes placing blocks much easier.
        • Placing blocks: hold A to keep placing blocks in a row. Hold ZL to walk sideways while you place, which makes straight walls easy.
        • The day resets at 5 AM. Shop stock, Mosslax's buff and your Dream Island trip all refresh then.
        • Pick up everything: press Y to grab small bits on the ground. Wood scraps, stones and seeds add up fast.
        Tip: Learn new moves and follow the story before spending a lot of time decorating. Each move opens up new things to do.

        🌾 Farming
        1. Help Drilbur in eastern Withered Wasteland. It teaches Rototiller and gives you 5 Bean Seeds.
        2. Use Rototiller on grass or dirt to make a farm plot.
        3. Hold the seeds from your bag, aim at the plot and press A to plant them.
        4. Water them with Water Gun. Watered crops grow one stage a day and are ready in about 3 days.
        5. Each ripe plant gives 3 crops. After harvesting, water the plant again and it regrows, so there's no need to replant.
        Tip: Water the dirt before using Rototiller, because it won't work on dry dirt.

        For every crop, berry and the best farm setups, see How do I… and Busy-bee setups.

        🏡 Homes
        • Building kits are sold in the PC Shop inside any Pokémon Center. Place the kit, add the materials it needs, and have a Build Pokémon (like Machop or Timburr) build it.
        • You can also build your own house: walls at least 2 blocks high, a door, and at least 3 pieces of furniture. Up to 4 Pokémon can share a house.

        To see what each Pokémon is good at, check Pokémon skills explained.

        🤝 Helpers: who to bring for what

        How to get Pokémon to help

        • Bring one along: walk up to a Pokémon friend and press Up on the D-pad, or talk to it and choose "Follow me!". Up to 5 can follow you at once.
        • Send one home: press Down near it. Hold Up to send everyone home at once.
        • Hungry Pokémon won't follow. Feed them something first.
        • Followers help by themselves: just use the object, for example a campfire or a tree, and a follower with the right skill does the job.
        • Give them materials: talk to a Pokémon and choose "Look at this!" to hand over things it can work on, like logs for a Chop Pokémon.

        🐝 Busy-bee setups (free items every day)

        The trick: Litter Pokémon drop items near their home every day, even when you're away. A Gather Pokémon picks them up and puts them in a Community Box. You just come back and empty the box.

        The basic setup

        1. Build the habitat for a Litter Pokémon and make friends with it.
        2. Craft a Community Box and put it right next to that habitat.
        3. Move a Gather Pokémon (like Machop or Machoke) in nearby.
        4. It keeps sweeping up the drops into the box. Check it every day or two.

        Great combos

        • 🍯 Honey farm: Combee (and Vespiquen) + a Gather Pokémon + a Community Box.
        • ⚙️ Iron farm (Rocky Ridges): Glimmet or Glimmora + a Gather Pokémon + a Community Box. Add a Burn Pokémon to smelt the ore into ingots.
        • 🧱 Brick farm: Clodsire or Trapinch drops clay. Hand it to Charmander or Torchic to bake into bricks (you need 40 for the ending).
        • 🪓 Lumber farm: Haxorus drops logs and chops them into lumber itself.
        • 🔁 Rubbish to iron: Grimer or Garbodor drops rubbish. Trubbish (Recycle) turns it into Iron Ore.

        🌱 Self-watering crop farm

        1. Dig a water hole at least 2×2 so it refills itself, and cover it with a plank.
        2. Put a Sprinkler on top. It waters a diamond shape of soil around it, up to 5 blocks out.
        3. Till a diamond of soil around the sprinkler and plant your crops. Square fields leave the corners dry.
        4. Keep a Grow Pokémon (Bulbasaur, Rowlet, Oddish) living next to the farm to speed things up.
        5. No sprinkler yet? A Water Pokémon living near a full water basin waters nearby crops for you.
        About berries: no Pokémon shakes the trees for you, so berries are still a quick A-press each. Plant all your berry trees in one spot near home so collecting them takes seconds.

        🧠 Handy tricks

        Storage & carrying

        • Move a whole box in one go: hit a storage box with Rock Smash and everything inside drops out. Hold Y to suck up the box and all its items, then put the box down wherever you like. (Newer versions of the game also have a "move all" option inside boxes.)
        • Boxes next to the crafting bench: any storage box touching a crafting bench feeds it materials directly, so there's no need to carry them. You can fit up to 4 big boxes around one bench.
        • Big Storage Box: craft it with 3 Pokémetal. It holds 60 items, 3× a normal box.
        • Sort in one tap: open your bag or a box and hold X.
        • Gulpin = a backpack: bring Gulpin along and it carries extra items for you.
        • Normal boxes don't share between areas. The special storage device (about 3,000 Life Coins, in update 2.0) does, so it's worth saving up for.
        • Hold Y to vacuum up everything on the ground in front of you, instead of one item at a time.

        Finding Pokémon

        • Fly to a missing Pokémon: open the Pokédex, press + on the Pokémon you want, and show it to a friend with the Fly or Teleport skill (like Pidgey). It takes you to that Pokémon. You might still need Honey to attract it.
        • Look for sparkles: sparkles near objects hint at a habitat you can build there. Sparkles in water usually mean an item.
        • Feed Mosslax something Spicy on days you're hunting for new Pokémon, so more show up.
        • The game uses the real clock. Some Pokémon only appear at certain times, so try checking habitats in the morning and again at night.

        Happiness shortcuts

        • Ask them: talk to a Pokémon and choose "How's your comfort level?". It tells you what it wants added to its home.
        • Quick boost for new friends: check its favourite flavour in the Pokédex and give it a berry or dish of that flavour.

        Building & time savers

        • Hold A to place blocks in a row, and hold ZL to walk sideways while placing.
        • Start big builds before bed. Construction runs on the real clock, so it finishes overnight.
        • Cook with Chef Dente following you. There's a chance of double food. Leave Chef Dente at home only when a recipe needs a different helper.
        • Vines on walls: aim Leafage at a wall to grow vines. Powered-up Leafage (after a salad) makes a different style.
        • Swap followers at any habitat whenever you like, so match the team to the job.

        Floating security camera (fun one)

        1. Stack blocks under yourself to climb up.
        2. At the height you want, place a block beside you and put a security camera on it.
        3. Break the block under the camera, then break the pillar you climbed.
        4. The camera stays floating in the air, giving you a bird's-eye view. Don't put it too high, or it can't see habitats.

        😊 Making Pokémon happy

        Happy Pokémon raise the area's Environment Level, which unlocks new shop items and moves the story along. You need level 5 in all 4 areas to finish the game.

        See what a Pokémon likes

        Open the Pokédex, pick the Pokémon and go to the "Specialties and Likes" tab. It lists their favourite things (like "Cute stuff" or "Wooden stuff") and their favourite food flavour.

        Quick ways to raise happiness

        • Help the saddest Pokémon first. Often a berry, a toy or a bed is enough to cheer one up.
        • Move them into proper houses. Houses make them happier than open habitats.
        • Decorate with their favourite things, and remove anything they dislike.
        • Feed them dishes in their favourite flavour. This works much better than raw food.
        • Do their requests and play their mini-games.
        • Turn dry mud into grass with Water Gun and Leafage. Greener land raises the level too.

        🎀 Decorating ideas
        • Decorate to their taste: check each Pokémon's likes and theme their house around them, for example a cosy wooden cottage or a cute pastel room. It looks lovely and makes them happier.
        • Use habitats as decorations: a picnic set (2 seats, table, picnic basket) makes a cute garden corner and attracts Pikachu. A restaurant setup (seat, table, menu board, plated food) makes a café and attracts Pawmi.
        • Add lights everywhere: they make things feel cosy, and some habitats need a light.
        • Furniture works indoors and outdoors. Benches, lamps and flower pots along paths make a town feel lived-in.
        • Recolour things: later on you can paint items to match a colour theme.
        • Shop stock changes daily, so check the PC Shop often for new furniture.

        💰 Life Coins (money)
        • The easiest way to earn coins is Challenges on the Pokémon Center PC. Remember to go back to the PC to collect your reward.
        • Do the Stamp Rally: get one stamp a day from a PC, then trade in the full card for a big pile of coins.
        • Crafted items sell for more than raw materials.
        • Good first buy: Packing Tips from the shop, which give you a bigger bag. New ones appear as you unlock new areas.

        🍞 Cooking with Chef Dente

        Rescuing Chef Dente (Rocky Ridges)

        1. Collect 3 Wheat from the dry patches near the waterfall.
        2. Bake Simple Bread in a Bread Oven. You need a fire Pokémon (Burn helper) to light the oven.
        3. Eat the bread to power up Cut, then cut the chain holding his barrel.

        How food works

        Eating a cooked dish refills all your PP and powers up one move:

        FoodCooked onMain ingredientPowers up
        SaladCutting BoardLeafLeafage (grows grass in a + shape)
        SoupCooking PotFresh WaterWater Gun (bigger spray)
        BreadBread OvenWheatCut (cuts tougher things, from further away)
        Hamburger SteakFrying PanBeanRock Smash (breaks hard rock, gets gold and iron)
        Recipes worth knowing
        • Simple Salad: leaf + anything
        • Simple Soup: fresh water + any 2
        • Simple Bread: wheat + any 2. Needed to free Chef Dente.
        • Simple Hamburger Steak: bean + any 3. Needed to break into the legendary caves.
        • Vibrant Hamburger Steak: bean + potato + salad + any. Gives a bigger Rock Smash boost.
        Tip: Keep a couple of dishes in your bag at all times. They're your emergency PP refill.

        🍽️ Mosslax's daily treat

        After the Bleak Beach story, you can leave one dish a day at Mosslax's altar. The dish's flavour decides what bonus you get for the rest of the day:

        • Spicy: more Pokémon show up in habitats (great when hunting new ones)
        • Sweet: find more ancient artefacts when digging
        • Bitter: more rare items
        • Dry: more rare bird Pokémon (Mosslax's favourite)
        • Sour: better items in the shop
        • Plain: make friends with Pokémon faster

        🏁 Getting ready for the ending
